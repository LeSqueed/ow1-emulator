# 1.0.0

## General changes

* Role passives disabled through the workshop.

* Added a global health passive. After not taking damage for 6 seconds, you regain 8 HPS + 5% of your maximum health.

## Domina

### Health
100 health
250 shields 

_The shields she regains from landing abilities based on live damage numbers. Might have to keep an eye on if this amount of shield health is healthy with that amount of regen_

### Photon Magnum

* Primary fire beam damage reduced 30 damage total for a full beam hit.
* Primary fire hitscan shot changed from 60 to 50. 

_With the reduced health pools. This moves more power into the hitscan shot. Making it so that hitting the shot is rewarded more and giving people more time to react when being targeted. Have to keep an eye on how this feels for both parties._

### Barrier array

* Changed segment health to 250

### Sonic Repulsor

* Removed wall impact damage and stun
* Set base damage to 55

_To compensate for the stun and wall damage being removed, the initial damage is signifanctly higher. This should keep it a strong close ranged ability while removing the more annoying and random feeling parts of the ability._

### Crystal Charge 

* Total damage over time 35
* Explosing damage scaling changed to 85-20

### Panopticon

* Ult cost 2000
* Piercing damage on direct hit 40
* Implosion damage 230

_Ult shields are scaled the same as her shield array. With them having more health, the damage is slightly reduced while still keeping it lethal to most non tank heroes._

## Jetpack Cat

_Trying to get rid of the most frustrating pats of the heroes kit while still keeping them viable. Fear right now with these changes is that the hero feels boring... but fixing that is probably a bigger task based on the core design Blizzard went with. I will take balanced and boring over balanced and annoying._

### Health

175 health

_Lower health due to the mobility of the hero. The changes also motivate a more team orientated playstyle with less risks._

### Biotic Pawjectiles

* Center pellet damage reduced from 8 to 6
* Paw pellet damage reduced from 4 to 3
* Healing per shot reduced from 24 to 18

### Frenetic Flight

* Speed buff reduced from 240.4% to 150%
* Fuel regeneration rate increased from 25% to 35%

_Trading more up time for lower speed. Encouraging to play smarter since you are easier to punish if you do use your speed into a risky position_

### Lifeline

* Healing reduced to 15
* Speed buff reduced to 25%
* Maximum active duration capped at 5 seconds
* Cooldown increased to 4 seconds

_Lifeline now has a cooldown. This moves it more into an ability to quickly reposition an ally, similar to life petal or pull. While still being more flexible in where you can move them._

### Purr

* Damage reduced to 10
* Healing increased from 30 to 35

_Purr loses a bit of damage. Playing closer with the team should enable more use out of the knock back element and increased healing._

### Catnapper

* Ult cost reduced from 2400 to 1950
